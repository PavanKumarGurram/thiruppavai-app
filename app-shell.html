About Thiruppavai

Composed by Andal (Kodhai), the only woman among the twelve Vaishnava Azhwars, the Thiruppavai is a garland of thirty Tamil verses sung during Margazhi (mid-December to mid-January). Imagining herself as a cowherd girl of Ayarpadi, Andal wakes her companions before dawn, and together they sing awake Nandagopa, Yasodha, Krishna and Nappinnai — seeking from the Lord the parai, the drum of grace.

Each verse is shown in traditional Tamil script with Roman transliteration and a plain-English meaning. Raga associations follow common concert tradition. Tamil text: traditional printed editions (via Tamil Wikisource). English meanings: original summaries after P. R. Ramachander's verse translation.
