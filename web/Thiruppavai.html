About this collection

Andal's Thiruppavai is a sequence of thirty devotional Tamil pasurams addressed to Narayana, traditionally recited during Margazhi. The Tamil text is presented with readable word spacing; sandhi, spacing, and some spellings vary by edition. The text was collated from Project Madurai's edition at Tamilnation, Tamil Wikisource, and the Valmiki Ramayanam recitation text.

The daily verse

This app uses the fixed calendar you requested: December 16 is day 1 and January 14 is day 30, according to your device's local date. Outside this window, a clearly labelled daily reflection cycles through the thirty verses. Actual Tamil-calendar dates can vary; this is not an astronomical calendar.

Reading the Roman text

The transliteration maps the Tamil letters, rather than imitating one recitation accent. Long vowels are ā, ī, ū, ē, ō; ai and au are diphthongs. ṭ, ṇ, and ḷ are retroflex sounds; ḻ is the Tamil “zh”; ṟ is the strong r; ṉ distinguishes ன from ந (n). c represents ச, and t represents த; their spoken sounds change with context. Word spacing follows the Tamil. The simplified Roman titles make familiar spellings easy to search.

Understanding the meaning

The English meanings are original prose renderings informed by the Tamil and P. R. Ramachander's translation on Hindupedia. They give the narrative sense, not the full range of traditional commentary. “Parai” literally names a drum; in the devotional reading, the sought gift points toward the Lord's grace and service to him, made explicit in verse 29.

Your portable reading copy

No account, tracking, or server is needed. Text, search, fonts, and themes work offline. Bookmarks, reading progress, theme, and text size are kept in this open tab. Choose “Save your copy” to download a fresh HTML file containing those choices. Open that new file next time. The app cannot silently overwrite the original attachment.

Individual audio recitations

Each pasuram links to its corresponding temple recording in SriPedia's Thiruppavai collection, hosted by ibiblio. Recordings are streamed directly from the host after you press play, not copied into this file. Playback requires internet access and depends on the host's availability. The “Recording source” link opens the source page for the current verse if in-app playback is unavailable.

Play/pause, seeking, speed, and repeat affect only the current verse. Navigation stops playback; no recitation starts automatically. These are traditional recordings, not generated speech. Recording rights remain with their respective owners; source availability does not imply a license to redistribute the recordings.
