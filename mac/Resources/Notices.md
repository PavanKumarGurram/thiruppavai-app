# Content and font notices

## Tamil text

The ancient verses are attributed to Andal. This reading edition was collated from [Project Madurai at Tamilnation](https://tamilnation.org/sathyam/east/pirapantam/mp005baandaal), [Tamil Wikisource](https://ta.wikisource.org/wiki/திருப்பாவை), and the [Valmiki Ramayanam recitation text](https://valmikiramayanam.in/?p=8432). Word spacing has been regularized and typographical corrections applied; textual spellings vary across editions.

The originating Project Madurai header is retained below. Its reference to Nithyasree Mahadevan belongs to that source page, not to the recordings streamed in this app.

## Roman text and English meanings

Roman transliteration is an original letter-based transformation of the included Tamil. The English prose renderings are original summaries informed by the Tamil and [P. R. Ramachander's translation on Hindupedia](https://www.hindupedia.com/en/Thiruppavai); they are not a reproduction of that translation or a comprehensive theological commentary.

## Audio

Each audio mapping was extracted from the corresponding pasuram page in [SriPedia's Thiruppavai collection on ibiblio](https://ibiblio.org/sripedia/ebooks/tpv/vstp01.html). All thirty source-page and stream URLs are stored in verses.json.

Recordings stream from their original host after the user initiates playback. No audio files are included in the app or source archive. The app does not claim ownership of the recordings, permission to redistribute them, or a guarantee of continued host availability. Obtain any necessary permissions and review host terms before public distribution; a freely reachable recording is not automatically licensed for redistribution.
